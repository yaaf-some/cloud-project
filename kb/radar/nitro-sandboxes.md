# Nitro Sandboxes

**What it is:** AWS hardware-isolated microVM per pod/task (EKS, ECS Managed Instances, EC2), launched Nov 9, 2026, at no extra charge. Opt in on EKS Auto Mode with `runtimeClassName: isolated-sandbox`. No GPU pods.

**Why it matters for Runway:** The cloud half of Lab 05. Cheap and one line to adopt.
