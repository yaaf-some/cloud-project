**What it is:** Google-donated CNCF Sandbox runtime: zero-trust sandboxing for very large numbers of agents on Kubernetes, gVisor or microVM only, suspend/resume onto warm pods. kagent's default backend. Pre-1.0 outside GKE.

**Why it matters for Runway:** An alternative to OpenShell in Lab 05. Worth a 'Why not X?' paragraph.
